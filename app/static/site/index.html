Security and privacy

Built for real company data.

Your information stays yours – and stays with the people who should see it.

Reads only

Cortex never sends, posts, edits or deletes anything in your accounts.

Permissions checked first

Access is checked before anything reaches the AI, so an answer can only use what the person asking may see.

Official, encrypted sign-ins

Connections use each provider's own sign-in. Access keys are stored encrypted and can be disconnected anytime.

Not used for training

Answers are written by a leading AI model under business terms – your data is not used to train it.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which tools can Cortex connect to?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Outlook mail and calendar, OneDrive, Slack, WhatsApp Business, Facebook Pages, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn (by import), any website, and files: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, emails and audio or video recordings. Personal and group WhatsApp chats can be imported from the phone.

Do employees see everything?

No. You decide who can find each source – everyone, or specific departments. Answers use only what the person asking is allowed to see. New employees can be limited to the Learning Center.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses connect their main accounts and website in an afternoon. Importing runs in the background, and you can start asking right away.

Does it work in Hebrew?

Yes. Ask in Hebrew or English and Cortex answers in the language you asked in.

Will it make things up?

It's built not to. Cortex answers only from your information, shows the source of each answer, and says plainly when it couldn't find something.

What does it cost?

A simple monthly plan that grows with your team. We're onboarding our first customers now – talk to us for early access.
