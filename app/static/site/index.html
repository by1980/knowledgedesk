Security and privacy

Your information stays yours.

Designed from day one for real company data.

Reads only

KnowledgeDesk never sends, posts, edits or deletes anything in your accounts.

Permissions enforced first

Each person can only get answers from information their department is allowed to see. New employees see only their learning path.

Encrypted sign-ins

Connections use each provider's official sign-in. Access keys are stored encrypted and can be disconnected at any time.

Not used for training

Answers are written by a leading AI model under business terms – your data is not used to train it.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which tools can I connect?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Outlook mail and calendar, OneDrive, Slack, WhatsApp Business, Facebook Pages, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn (by import), any website, and files: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, emails and audio or video recordings. Personal and group WhatsApp chats can be imported from the phone.

Do employees see everything?

No. You decide who can find each source – everyone, or specific departments. Answers are built only from what the person asking is allowed to see. New employees can be limited to the Learning Center only.

How long does it take to set up?

Most businesses connect their main accounts and website in under an hour. Importing runs in the background, and you can start asking right away.

Does it work in Hebrew?

Yes. Ask in Hebrew or English and the answer comes back in the language you asked in – even when the sources are in the other language.

Will it make things up?

It's built not to. It answers only from your information, shows the source for every fact, and says plainly when it couldn't find something.

What does it cost?

A simple monthly plan, starting with small teams and growing with you. We're onboarding our first customers now – talk to us for early access.
