LinkedIn

Your company page's posts and their numbers. LinkedIn only lets approved partners connect directly, so import its export file – it takes a minute.

How to get the file from LinkedIn
  1. On LinkedIn, open your company page as an admin.
  2. Click Analytics → Content.
  3. Choose the time range (for example the last 365 days), then click Export. LinkedIn downloads an Excel file.
  4. Click Import LinkedIn file here and choose that file. Do it again every month or so to keep the numbers fresh.

Chats

WhatsApp

Your WhatsApp Business number – every message to and from it, as it happens

Personal and group chats, and older history: import from the phone

WhatsApp doesn't let any app connect to personal or group chats, or read history. Export them instead – it takes a minute:

  1. On the phone, open the chat or group.
  2. iPhone: tap the chat's name → Export chat. Android: ⋮ → More → Export chat. Choose Attach media to include voice notes.
  3. Save or send the file to this computer, then click Import a chat.
